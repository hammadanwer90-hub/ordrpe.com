How Shoppish membership works

From your first message to delivery in Pakistan — facilitation, clear quotes, and terms you can review before you pay.

01
Choose your tier

Start free (Tier 3) or upgrade to X, S, or Y for lower fees, better exchange rates, and premium perks like the Shoppish Card.

02
Send your order

Share the product link, price, and weight on WhatsApp — or shop yourself with the Shoppish Card on Tier S and above.

03
Pay upfront

100% payment is required before we confirm. Your landed cost includes checkout amount, service fee, carry charge, and any shipping you add.

We facilitate the purchase and arrange transport through independent third parties. Delivery timelines vary and are estimates only — never assume a date unless confirmed in writing.

Membership details

Shoppish Card & membership

Key details for members. Please review all policies before joining or placing orders.

Launch offer

Membership pricing

  • Launch rate: $36/month (active membership required)
  • Regular rate: $100/month after launch period
  • Tier X, S, and Y have their own monthly fees — see tier cards above
  • Free tier (Tier 3) has no monthly subscription

Shoppish Card

  • Processing: $100 one-time (or 4 installments)
  • First deposit: free
  • After that: $5 per $250 (waived on $2,500 multiples)
  • Overdraft: $35 fee if balance goes negative
  • Inactivity: deactivates after 25 days (reactivation may apply)
  • Deposit fees apply to total account usage

What each tier includes

  • Free: 15% fee · $25/kg · 295 rate
  • Tier X: 10% fee · $22/kg · 290 rate
  • Tier S: 8% fee · Shoppish Card · in-store shopping
  • Tier Y: 6% fee · virtual card · Apple Pay · rate lock

Ordering essentials

  • 100% upfront payment before confirmation
  • No cancellations after order is confirmed
  • Refunds only when eligible — misuse may remove privilege
  • Third-party shipping — no guaranteed delivery dates
  • Do not place urgent or deadline-based orders
FAQ

Common questions

Which tier should I pick? +
Occasional shoppers: start on Free. Regular monthly orders: Tier X. Heavy shoppers and card users: Tier S or Y.
Is the monthly fee included in the comparator? +
Landed cost per order is shown; each card lists the tier monthly fee separately.
What is checkout amount? +
Product price plus checkout charges (10.7%), shown as one line.
How is weight calculated? +
Rounded up to the next whole kilogram for carry charges.
Can I change tiers? +
Yes — contact us on WhatsApp. Upgrades apply to new orders going forward.
